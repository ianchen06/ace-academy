---
skill: Footwork
title: Last-Three-Steps Tuning
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a partner or feeder'
goal: Replace one big lunge with several small adjustment steps before contact.
---

1. Have a partner feed easy balls near you — deliberately not far enough away to require a sprint.
2. Before every contact, take at least three small stutter steps, even though the ball is close.
3. Count the steps out loud ("one, two, three, hit") so the rhythm becomes audible.
4. Do 20 feeds per side, then rally and notice whether the small steps survive live play.
