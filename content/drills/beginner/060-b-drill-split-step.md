---
skill: Footwork
title: Mirror Split Step
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: A partner (no ball needed)
goal: Groove the timing of the split step reaction.
---

1. Stand facing a partner a few feet apart, both in ready position.
2. Partner randomly points left, right, or forward without warning.
3. React with a split step the instant they move, then shuffle explosively in the pointed direction.
4. Switch roles after 10 reps and repeat for 3 rounds each.
