---
skill: Footwork
title: Shuffle and Recover
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, 2 cones'
goal: >-
  Learn to move sideways and return to the middle without crossing your feet
  under pressure.
---

1. Place two cones about 12 feet apart with the centre mark between them.
2. Side-shuffle to one cone, shadow-swing a forehand, then shuffle back to the centre mark and split step.
3. Repeat to the other side for a backhand shadow swing, staying low the whole time — your head should not bob up and down.
4. Do 8 shuffles per side, resting 30 seconds between sets, for 3 sets.
