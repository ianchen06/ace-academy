---
skill: Serve
title: Service Box Accuracy
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls'
goal: Build a reliable serve motion from a shorter distance.
---

1. Stand at the service line (not the baseline) facing the diagonal service box.
2. Serve 10 balls, focusing on smooth toss and full extension at contact.
3. Once you land 7+/10, move back halfway to the baseline and repeat.
4. Finish serving from the full baseline once comfortable.
