---
skill: Fundamentals
title: Bevel Hunt
duration: 5 minutes
equipment: Racquet only
goal: 'Learn to find any grip by feel, using the handle bevels as landmarks.'
---

1. Sit down with the racquet and run your index finger around the eight bevels of the handle, counting them out loud from bevel 1 (the flat top when the racquet is on edge) clockwise for a right-hander.
2. Place your base knuckle on bevel 2 and say "Continental", then bevel 3 and say "Eastern", bevel 4 "Semi-Western", bevel 5 "Western".
3. Close your eyes and have someone call out a grip; find it by feel, then open your eyes and check the knuckle position.
4. Score yourself out of 10 calls, and repeat until you get 9 or 10 right without looking.
