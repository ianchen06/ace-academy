---
skill: Serve
title: Toss Consistency Drill
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls
goal: Develop a repeatable, accurate ball toss.
---

1. Stand in your serve stance without swinging.
2. Toss the ball as if serving and let it drop to the ground without hitting it.
3. Mark or notice where each toss lands — aim for the same spot every time (slightly in front, in line with your front foot).
4. Do 20 tosses; count how many land within a small target area (e.g. a towel on the ground).
