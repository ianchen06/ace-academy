---
skill: Forehand
title: Cooperative Forehand Rally
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a partner
goal: Sustain a forehand-to-forehand rally with a partner.
---

1. Stand at the service line with your partner, both using only forehands.
2. Rally cooperatively, aiming for the middle of the court at a comfortable pace.
3. Count consecutive shots as a pair and try to beat your record.
4. Once comfortable, move back to the baseline and repeat.
