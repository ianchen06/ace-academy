---
skill: Forehand
title: Wall Rally
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a practice wall'
goal: Groove a consistent forehand swing path with constant repetition.
---

1. Stand about 8-10 feet from a wall or backboard.
2. Feed yourself a ball and hit soft forehands, letting it bounce once before each swing.
3. Focus on a low-to-high swing path and finishing high over your shoulder.
4. Try to string together 20 consecutive controlled forehands without a big miss.
