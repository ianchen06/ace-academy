---
skill: Forehand
title: 'Take It Early: Rise-Ball Drill'
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a feeder or ball machine'
goal: >-
  Build the compact, straight-arm Eastern forehand that lets you take the ball
  early, on the rise.
---

1. Stand a step inside the baseline with an Eastern grip, and have the feeder hit with enough pace that the ball arrives just after the bounce, not after it has floated up.
2. Turn the instant you read forehand and keep the backswing short — a small loop, hands set high, no long wind-up — so you are ready before the ball reaches you.
3. Contact the ball at waist height with a long, extended arm, then hold a full, high finish over the opposite shoulder rather than a wrapped one.
4. Every fifth ball, shadow-swing a grip change to Continental immediately after the finish, simulating a transition to net.
