---
skill: Tactics
title: Crosscourt Consistency Rally
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a partner
goal: Build rally tolerance and shot selection discipline.
---

1. Rally exclusively crosscourt (forehand to forehand, or backhand to backhand) with a partner.
2. Aim for depth (past the service line) on every shot rather than pace.
3. Count consecutive crosscourt shots as a pair; reset if the ball lands short or out.
4. Try to reach a rally of 15+ shots three times in a row.
