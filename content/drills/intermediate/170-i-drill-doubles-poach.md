---
skill: Doubles
title: Poach & Communicate
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, 3 other players'
goal: Practice net poaching and verbal communication in doubles.
---

1. Play doubles points starting with a simple feed to the returner.
2. The server's partner looks for any crosscourt ball to poach and put away at net.
3. Call "mine!" clearly before crossing to poach.
4. Play 10 points, rotating the poaching role among all four players.
