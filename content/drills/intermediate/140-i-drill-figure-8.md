---
skill: Footwork
title: Figure-8 Cone Footwork
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: 4-6 cones
goal: Improve lateral agility and recovery speed.
---

1. Set cones in a figure-8 pattern across roughly one side of the court.
2. Weave through the cones using side-shuffle and crossover steps, staying low.
3. Complete 5 full figure-8 laps, resting 30 seconds between laps.
4. Finish each lap with a split step and a shadow swing to simulate a real point.
