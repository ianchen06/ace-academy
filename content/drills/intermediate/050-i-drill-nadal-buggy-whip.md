---
skill: Forehand
title: Buggy-Whip Crosscourt Loops
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a feeder or ball machine'
goal: >-
  Groove the high, open-stance, lasso finish that generates heavy topspin on
  running crosscourt forehands.
---

1. Set a Semi-Western grip and have the feeder loop balls that bounce between shoulder and head height into your forehand corner.
2. Load fully into the outside leg from an open stance, take a high, exaggerated loop backswing, and brush steeply up the back of the ball.
3. Let the finish whip up over your head on the same side (the "lasso" or "buggy whip") instead of across your body, and use the same outside-leg push to recover.
4. Hit 3 sets of 10, then hit 10 more down-the-line balls finishing with the standard windshield-wiper across the body, and compare how each finish changes the ball's trajectory.
