---
skill: Footwork
title: Spider Run
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: '5 balls or cones, a stopwatch'
goal: Build repeatable multi-directional movement and measure it over time.
---

1. Place 5 balls: one at each baseline corner, one at each singles sideline/service line junction, and one at the centre of the T.
2. Starting at the centre mark, sprint to each ball in turn, return it to the centre mark, and go again — all 5 in one continuous run.
3. Time the full circuit, rest 90 seconds, and repeat 4 times.
4. Log your best time each session; a shrinking time means your first step and recovery are getting faster.
