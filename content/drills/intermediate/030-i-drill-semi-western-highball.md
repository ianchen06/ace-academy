---
skill: Forehand
title: Semi-Western High-Ball Windshield Wipers
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a feeder or ball machine
goal: Groove the steep low-to-high path and across-the-body finish of the Semi-Western forehand.
---

1. Have the feeder loop balls that bounce between shoulder and head height.
2. Set the Semi-Western grip, drop the racquet head well below the ball, and brush up steeply, finishing across the body rather than over the shoulder.
3. Aim for a target 3 feet inside the baseline, with at least 6 feet of net clearance — the ball should drop sharply.
4. Hit 3 sets of 10, counting how many land deep in the target zone.
