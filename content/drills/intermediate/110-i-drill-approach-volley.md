---
skill: Net Play
title: Approach-Volley-Volley Sequence
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a feeder'
goal: Link an approach shot to two clean finishing volleys.
---

1. Have a feeder feed a short ball from the baseline.
2. Hit an approach shot and move to the net, splitting as the feeder returns.
3. Hit a controlled first volley deep, then close in further for a putaway second volley.
4. Repeat 10 times, alternating forehand and backhand approaches.
