---
skill: Backhand
title: Slice & Advance
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a partner or feeder'
goal: Combine a slice backhand with forward movement to the net.
---

1. Have a partner feed a short ball to your backhand side.
2. Hit a low, skidding slice approach shot and immediately move forward toward the net.
3. Split step at the service line, ready to volley the next ball.
4. Repeat 10 times, alternating which side the approach is hit to.
