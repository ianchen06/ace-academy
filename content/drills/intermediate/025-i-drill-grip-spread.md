---
skill: Fundamentals
title: Bunched vs Spread Hand Test
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a partner or feeder
goal: Find out whether spreading your index finger up the handle steadies your racquet, using deliberate off-centre contact as the test.
---

1. Hold your normal volley grip with the fingers bunched together and have your partner feed 10 balls at the net, aiming a third of them off-centre toward the frame.
2. Note how often the racquet twists in your hand and where those volleys land.
3. Separate your index finger from the middle finger, lay its pad diagonally up the handle, and take the same 10 feeds again.
4. Compare the two sets on one thing only: how much the racquet turned on the off-centre balls.
5. Repeat the comparison on 10 second serves, watching instead for whether your forearm still rotates freely through contact.
6. Keep whichever hand shape won at the net, and whichever won on the serve — they do not have to be the same one.
