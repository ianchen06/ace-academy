---
skill: Forehand
title: Topspin Target Rally
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, cones or targets, a partner or feeder
goal: Add controlled topspin while hitting to a target.
---

1. Set a target zone deep in the court, about 3 feet inside the baseline.
2. Rally forehands and backhands, focusing on a low-to-high swing path that clears the net by at least 3 feet.
3. Count how many balls land in the deep target zone out of 10 attempts.
4. Repeat for 3 rounds, trying to increase your target-zone success rate each round.
