---
skill: Serve
title: Kick Serve Toss & Brush
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls
goal: Groove the toss position and racquet path for a kick serve.
---

1. Toss the ball slightly further behind your head than a flat serve, more overhead and to the left (for righties).
2. Shadow swing brushing up the back-left of an imaginary ball, finishing across your body.
3. Serve 10 kick serves focusing on a high, safe net clearance and heavy bounce on landing.
4. Use this serve as your second serve in a practice set.
