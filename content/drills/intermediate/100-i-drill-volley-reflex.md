---
skill: Net Play
title: Reflex Volley Exchange
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a partner
goal: Sharpen hands and reaction time at net.
---

1. Stand at the service line facing a partner also at their service line.
2. Volley the ball back and forth firmly (no bounce), keeping the racquet head up.
3. Gradually increase pace as both players get comfortable.
4. Try to sustain 20+ consecutive volleys without an error.
