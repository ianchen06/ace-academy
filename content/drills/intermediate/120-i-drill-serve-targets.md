---
skill: Serve
title: Serve to Three Targets
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, 3 cones or markers
goal: 'Develop directional control: wide, body, and down the T.'
---

1. Place 3 cones in each service box: one wide, one central (body), one down the T.
2. Serve 5 balls at each target from the deuce court, then the ad court.
3. Score 2 points for hitting the target cone area, 1 point for landing in the correct box.
4. Track your score across sessions to measure improvement.
