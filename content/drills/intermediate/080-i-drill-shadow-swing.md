---
skill: Fundamentals
title: Low-to-High Shadow Swings
duration: 5 minutes
equipment: Racquet only
goal: Isolate and feel the topspin swing path without ball contact.
---

1. Stand in a split stance and shadow-swing a forehand slowly, exaggerating the low-to-high path.
2. Pause at the "low" point (racquet below ball height) and the "high" finish to feel the full range.
3. Do 15 slow reps, then 10 at full speed.
4. Repeat the sequence for your backhand.
