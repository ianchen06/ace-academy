---
skill: Footwork
title: 'Neutral, Semi-Open, Open'
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a feeder'
goal: Choose a stance based on where the ball is instead of defaulting to one.
---

1. Take 10 feeds right at you and hit from a neutral (closed) stance, stepping across with the front foot.
2. Take 10 feeds a couple of steps wide and hit from a semi-open stance, front foot angled toward the net post.
3. Take 10 feeds that pull you well outside the sideline and hit from a fully open stance, loading and pushing off the outside leg.
4. Finish with 20 random feeds where the feeder mixes all three distances, and let the ball choose your stance.
