---
skill: Footwork
title: Wide-Ball Recovery
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a feeder'
goal: Practice recovering to center after being pulled wide.
---

1. Have a feeder alternate feeding balls wide to your forehand and backhand.
2. After each shot, sprint back to the center mark before the next feed.
3. Do 10 reps per side, focusing on quick recovery steps, not the shot itself.
4. Increase feed pace as recovery speed improves.
