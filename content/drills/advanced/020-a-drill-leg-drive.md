---
skill: Serve
title: Leg Drive Isolation
duration: 10 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls'
goal: Feel and exaggerate the upward leg drive into the serve.
---

1. Serve from a deep knee bend, holding the bend for a full second before driving up.
2. Exaggerate the upward jump through contact for 10 serves.
3. Gradually reduce the pause while keeping the same explosive push.
4. Compare ball pace/spin before and after focusing on leg drive.
