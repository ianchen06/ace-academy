---
skill: Return of Serve
title: Compact Return Reaction Drill
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a partner or ball machine
goal: Shorten the backswing and improve return depth against fast serves.
---

1. Have a partner or ball machine feed serves at increasing pace.
2. Focus on an early, compact backswing and blocking the return deep crosscourt.
3. Track how many of 10 returns land past the service line.
4. Repeat against both flat and kick serve feeds.
