---
skill: Doubles
title: Australian Formation Points
duration: 20 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, 3 other players
goal: Practice executing and returning against the Australian (I) formation.
---

1. Serving team lines up in Australian formation (both players on the same side).
2. Server's partner fakes movement across the middle after the serve to disguise coverage.
3. Play out full points, focusing on taking away the returner's crosscourt return.
4. Switch which team serves in Australian formation every 4 points.
