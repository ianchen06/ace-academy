---
skill: Forehand
title: Grip Adaptation Gauntlet
duration: 20 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a feeder who can vary height and depth
goal: Adapt grip and contact height on the fly instead of forcing one pattern on every ball.
---

1. The feeder mixes four ball types at random: heavy topspin above the shoulder, low skidding slice, short low ball, and a flat drive at hip height.
2. Call out the grip you are using ("semi-western", "eastern") the instant you recognise the ball type.
3. Play each ball to a deep crosscourt target regardless of type, prioritising clean contact over pace.
4. Do 40 balls, then have the feeder tell you which ball type produced the most errors and drill only that one for 10 more.
