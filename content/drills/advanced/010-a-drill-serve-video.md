---
skill: Serve
title: Video Serve Analysis
duration: 20 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, phone/camera on a tripod'
goal: Identify kinetic chain breakdowns using video feedback.
---

1. Film your serve from the side and from behind at normal and slow-motion speed.
2. Check the trophy position: knees bent, racquet dropped low, tossing arm extended.
3. Check leg drive: are your knees driving upward through contact?
4. Hit 10 serves, review the video, and pick one specific checkpoint to correct on the next 10.
