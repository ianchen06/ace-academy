---
skill: Footwork
title: Wide-Ball Slide and Recover
duration: 20 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a feeder (clay court ideal, hard court with controlled steps)
goal: Turn a defensive wide ball into a balanced recovery instead of a scramble.
---

1. Feeder sends a ball that pulls you a full step outside the singles sideline.
2. On clay, slide into the shot with the outside leg; on hard court, brake with a strong outside-leg plant instead of sliding.
3. Hit a deep, high crosscourt ball to buy time, then push off the outside leg and recover past the centre mark before the next feed.
4. Do 8 reps per side, with the feeder shortening the gap between feeds each round.
