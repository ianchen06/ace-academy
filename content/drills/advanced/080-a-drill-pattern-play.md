---
skill: Tactics
title: Three-Ball Pattern Play
duration: 20 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a partner
goal: Train serve/rally patterns rather than improvised points.
---

1. Agree on a pattern beforehand, e.g. "crosscourt rally 3 balls, then go down the line."
2. Play out live points but committing to executing the pattern when the opportunity appears.
3. Track how often you successfully execute the pattern versus abandon it under pressure.
4. Rotate through 3 different patterns across a 20-minute session.
