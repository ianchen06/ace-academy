---
skill: Footwork
title: Explosive First Step Reactions
duration: 15 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, a partner, cones'
goal: Cut the gap between recognising a ball and pushing off toward it.
---

1. Stand on the baseline in ready position while a partner stands at the net with a ball in hand.
2. The partner points or throws to one side with no warning; split step and explode with a crossover first step, no shuffling in place.
3. Touch a cone 3 to 4 metres away, recover with crossover-then-shuffle steps, and reset within 2 seconds.
4. Do 6 sets of 8 reactions with 45 seconds rest — stop the set the moment your first step starts looking slow, since sloppy reps train sloppy movement.
