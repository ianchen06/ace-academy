---
skill: Forehand
title: Western Grip Heavy-Ball Sets
duration: 20 minutes
equipment: 'Racquet, balls, a ball machine or strong feeder'
goal: >-
  Produce a heavy, high-bouncing forehand from an extreme grip without losing
  depth.
---

1. Set the Western grip (base knuckle on bevel 5) and hit 10 forehands off shoulder-high feeds, contacting the ball well in front and above waist height.
2. Keep the racquet face closed through the drop and let the arm rotate rather than push — the finish wraps low across the body.
3. Alternate 5 heavy crosscourt balls with 5 flatter down-the-line drives, noting how much harder the flat ball is from this grip.
4. Finish with 10 balls fed low and short: adapt the grip toward Semi-Western or Eastern and compare the difficulty.
