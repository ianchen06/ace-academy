---
skill: Net Play
title: Approach-Split-Volley-Overhead
duration: 20 minutes
equipment: Racquet, balls, a feeder or partner
goal: Train the full transition sequence including defending a lob.
---

1. Feeder feeds a short ball; hit a down-the-line approach shot and move forward.
2. Split step inside the service line as the feeder returns (either a passing shot or a lob).
3. If it's a passing shot, hit a first volley for a winner; if it's a lob, retreat and hit an overhead.
4. Repeat 12 times, with the feeder randomly mixing passing shots and lobs.
