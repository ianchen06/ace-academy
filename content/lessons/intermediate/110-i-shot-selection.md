summary: Choosing the right shot for the situation instead of always going for winners.
tips:
  - When in doubt, hit crosscourt with margin over the net rather than for a line.
  - Ask "can I make this shot 8 out of 10 times?" before attempting it in a match.

Every shot has a risk/reward tradeoff.
From behind the baseline or on a low/difficult ball, prioritize a high-margin shot back to the middle of the court rather than an aggressive angle.

Look for a "green light" ball — one that lands short and/or high — before attacking.
Deep, low, or wide balls are better played with control (deep and crosscourt) than force.

Crosscourt shots have a longer distance over the lowest part of the net and more margin than down-the-line shots, making them the default "safe" rally pattern.

Track your own unforced errors during a practice set; most intermediate players lose points to their own mistakes far more than to opponents' winners.
