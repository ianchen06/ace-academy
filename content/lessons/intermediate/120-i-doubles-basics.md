The standard doubles formation is one player at net and one at the baseline (up-and-back), covering the court together — the net player poaches and finishes points, the baseline player builds them.

Communicate constantly: call "mine," "yours," "switch," and "out" so both partners always know who is covering which shot.

When your partner is pulled wide, both players should shift together (like connected by a rope) to cover the open court rather than leaving big gaps.

On serve, the server's partner stands at net ready to poach; on return, positioning shifts based on whether you are returning against a serve-and-volley opponent.
