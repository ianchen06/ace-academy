Topspin is created by brushing up the back of the ball with a low-to-high racquet path, making the ball rotate forward.
That rotation makes the ball dip down into the court, letting you swing harder while keeping the ball in.

To add topspin, drop the racquet head below the incoming ball on your backswing (racquet face slightly closed), then accelerate up and over the ball through contact, finishing high across your body or shoulder.

Topspin also makes the ball bounce higher and kick away from opponents, which is especially effective on clay and against players who like the ball at a comfortable strike zone.

Balance the amount of topspin to the situation: more topspin (steeper swing path) for high-margin rally balls, flatter and more topspin-controlled for putaway shots.
