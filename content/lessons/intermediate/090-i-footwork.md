title: Recovery Footwork & Court Positioning
summary: Getting to the ball efficiently and recovering to a strong position after.
tips:
  - Never watch your own shot — start recovering the instant you make contact.
  - Split step every single time your opponent is about to hit, no exceptions.

After hitting a shot, recover toward the center of the angles your opponent can hit — usually near the center mark, adjusted toward the side you just hit to since opponents often go crosscourt.

Use a mix of steps: crossover and side-shuffle steps for lateral movement, small adjustment steps to fine-tune distance right before contact, and a strong final push step into the shot.

Stay low with bent knees throughout rallies — a lower center of gravity lets you change direction faster and reach wide balls without lunging.

On defense, recover further behind the baseline to buy more time; on offense, look to move forward and take time away from your opponent.
