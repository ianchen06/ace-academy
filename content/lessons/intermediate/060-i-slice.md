title: The Slice Backhand
summary: A low, skidding shot for defense, approach, and variety.
tips:
  - Stay down through the shot; standing up early causes the ball to float long.
  - Use slice approach shots to stay low and buy time to get to the net.
drills:
  - i-drill-slice-approach
---

Slice is created with a high-to-low racquet path and a slightly open racquet face, imparting backspin that makes the ball float, skid low off the bounce, and stay close to the ground.

Grip: Continental works well for slice since it is the same grip used for serves and volleys — a big reason slice is a natural stroke to add once your serve grip is solid.

Common uses: neutralizing a fast or awkward ball, approaching the net behind a low shot that is hard to attack, and changing rhythm against a heavy topspin hitter.

Keep the swing compact with a firm wrist — slice relies on racquet face control more than raw racquet speed.
