title: How Tennis Scoring Works
summary: 'Points, games, sets, and the quirky love/15/30/40 system.'
tips:
  - >-
    Server calls the score before each point, server's score first (e.g.
    "30-15").
  - >-
    In doubles, teams alternate which partner serves each game, and receiving
    side alternates each point within a game.
---

Points within a game are counted 0 ("love"), 15, 30, 40, and game. If both players reach 40, it is called "deuce" — a player must then win by two clear points (advantage, then game).

A set is won by the first player to win 6 games, with a margin of at least 2 games (e.g. 6-4). If the set reaches 6-6, most formats play a tiebreak.

A tiebreak is played to 7 points (win by 2); points are counted normally (1, 2, 3...) rather than love/15/30/40, and players alternate serve every 2 points after the first point.

Matches are typically best-of-3 sets (first to win 2 sets) at most levels, or best-of-5 at some professional men's events.
