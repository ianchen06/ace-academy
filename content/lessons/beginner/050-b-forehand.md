tips:
  - Watch the ball onto the strings — most mishits happen because the head lifts too early.
  - Think "low to high" — the racquet should travel from below the ball to above it for natural topspin.

The forehand starts from ready position.
As the ball approaches, turn your shoulders and hips sideways to the net (unit turn) while bringing the racquet back in a smooth loop or straight backswing.

Step toward the ball with your front foot as you swing forward, making contact out in front of your body at waist height.
Lead with the racquet edge, then brush up and through the ball.

Finish with the racquet high, near your opposite shoulder — this "follow-through" confirms you accelerated through contact rather than pushing or steering the ball.

Footwork ties it together: small adjustment steps get you to the ball, and a stable base (feet grounded, knees bent) at contact gives you balance to redirect your weight into the shot.
