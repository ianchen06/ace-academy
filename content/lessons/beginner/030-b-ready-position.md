Ready position is the athletic stance you return to between every shot: feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent, weight on the balls of your feet, racquet held out in front with your non-dominant hand supporting the throat.

The split step is a small hop timed to land just as your opponent makes contact with the ball. Landing with both feet lightly on the ground (weight balanced) lets you push off explosively in any direction.

Without a split step, players are caught flat-footed and lose a full step of reaction time — the difference between reaching a ball comfortably and lunging for it.
