summary: 'Lines, boundaries, and what counts as a legal point.'
tips:
  - A ball landing on the line is considered "in" — lines are part of the court.
  - >-
    You get two chances to serve in (a first and second serve); missing both is
    a "double fault" and the point goes to your opponent.
---

A singles court is 78 feet long and 27 feet wide; doubles adds "alleys" on each side, widening the court to 36 feet. The net is 3 feet high at the center.

The court is divided by the net into two halves, each with a baseline (back boundary) and two service boxes near the net formed by the center service line and singles/doubles sidelines.

A serve must land in the diagonally opposite service box, clearing the net without touching it (a serve that clips the net cord and still lands in is a "let" and is replayed).

After the serve, players can hit the ball anywhere within the full singles (or doubles) court boundaries, and the ball is allowed to bounce once before being returned.
