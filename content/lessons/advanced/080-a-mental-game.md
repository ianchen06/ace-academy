Build a consistent between-point routine (roughly 20-25 seconds): a brief physical reset (towel, breath, racquet strings), then a clear tactical intention for the next point.

Play one point at a time. Dwelling on a previous error or a bad line call carries negative momentum into the next point — a short reset ritual (turning away from the court, exhaling) helps let it go.

Recognize momentum shifts (a run of games or a string of unforced errors) and respond deliberately: slow down your routine, simplify your shot selection, and rebuild with high-percentage tennis until you stabilize.

Separate process from outcome: focus on executing your game plan and shot quality rather than solely on the scoreboard, which reduces tension on big points.
