  - Aim your split step to land just after your opponent commits to their passing shot direction.

An approach shot is hit while moving forward, typically off a short ball, with the goal of following it to the net to volley the next shot for a winner.

Approach down the line more often than crosscourt — it takes the ball away from your opponent and cuts off their angle, while shortening the distance you need to cover to the net.

Split step just inside the service line as your opponent makes contact on the passing shot attempt — this is the single most important footwork detail in transition tennis.

Choose depth and pace over placement risk: a deep, penetrating approach that pins your opponent behind the baseline is more valuable than a low-percentage winner attempt.
