Elite serve power comes from the kinetic chain: legs drive up first, then hips rotate, then shoulders, then the arm and racquet whip through last — each segment accelerating the next.

A full trophy position (racquet dropped low behind the back, tossing arm up, knees bent, weight loaded on the back leg) stores elastic energy that releases explosively into the ball.

Pronation of the forearm through contact — not just wrist snap — is what produces the high racquet head speed pros use, combined with a full extension upward and outward at contact.

Leg drive (a strong knee bend followed by an explosive upward push, sometimes with both feet leaving the ground) adds significant free power without extra arm effort or injury risk.
