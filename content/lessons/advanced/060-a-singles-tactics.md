A classic pattern is "crosscourt to open the court, then go down the line" — repeated crosscourt shots push an opponent wide, and when they are stretched, a down-the-line shot into the open court is very hard to cover.

Play the percentages based on court geometry: the further you stand behind the baseline or outside the sideline, the more of the court you leave open — good opponents will exploit this deliberately.

Use the serve to set up the next shot (the "serve plus one" pattern): a wide serve to the deuce court, for example, opens the court for a forehand winner into the vacated space.

Identify and attack a weaker wing (often the backhand) by directing rally balls there repeatedly, forcing errors or defensive replies you can then attack.
