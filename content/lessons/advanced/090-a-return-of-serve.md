  - >-
    Against a big serve, prioritize simply getting a deep, controlled ball back
    in play over hitting a winner.

Against faster serves, shorten your backswing significantly — a compact "block and redirect" motion using the server's pace is far more reliable than a full swing.

Split step timing on return is earlier and lower than in rallies — time it to the server's contact, with weight already shifting forward to close in on the ball.

Return positioning should adapt to the server: step in against second serves to attack, and stand further back or move in for the kick serve's higher bounce.

Aim returns crosscourt and low over the net as a default to neutralize serve-and-volley opponents, reserving down-the-line returns for clear opportunities on weak second serves.
